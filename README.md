# Kn desk pet ( DESK-ORPH)
Desk Orph is a portable deskpet with a ESP32-S3 , a round GC9A01 display , TP4056 type C charger , a pazzive buzzer , Metal power switch  , 2 push buttons , a custom PCB and a 1000mAh rechargable battery . customize it  to make your very own desk pet :3

You can basically code it to code anything you want but im just making it do simple taks like showing the To-Do list , showing selective notifications from your phone and act as a alarm clock too.
